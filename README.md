# Customer-Shopping-Behaviour-Analytics

## 🏢 Industry Domain
Retail & Ecommerce

## 🏪 About the Company
Nexmart Retail Group is a leading retail company serving customers through both online and offline sales channels. The company offers a wide range of products across multiple categories and customer segments. Nexmart focuses on understanding customer needs, improving shopping experiences, increasing sales, building long-term customer relationships, and using consumer data to support better business decisions.



## 📌 Problem Statement
Nexmart Retail Group wants to better understand its customer's shopping behavior in order to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty. The management team has noticed changes in purchasing patterns across demographics, product categories, and sales channels (online vs offline). They are particularly interested in uncovering which factors, such as discounts, reviews, seasons, or payment preferences, drive consumer decisions and repeat purchases.

You are tasked with analyzing Nexmart Retail Group's consumer behavior dataset to answer the following overarching business question:

**"How can Nexmart Retail Group leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?"**


## 🎯 Objective
Analyze Nexmart Retail Group's consumer shopping data to identify purchasing trends and understand customer preferences. Determine how discounts, reviews, seasons, payment preferences, demographics, product categories, and sales channels influence decisions and repeat purchases to improve engagement, marketing, product strategies, sales, and loyalty.
